# NovaBank Operations and Fraud Team Runbook

> NovaBank is a fictional company. This document is sample data for demonstration purposes only.

## Failed or Delayed Transfers
If a customer reports a transfer that failed or has not arrived, first confirm whether the issue affects one customer or many customers. Check the payment status dashboard for known incidents. For a single customer, verify the account balance, the beneficiary details, the daily transfer limit, and whether the transfer is waiting for a compliance check. For widespread delays, do not ask each customer to repeat the transfer; escalate the suspected service incident to the Operations Team.

## Customer Mobile App Login Problems
If a customer cannot log in to the mobile app, first confirm that the app is updated to the latest version and that the phone has internet access. Then check whether the account is locked after repeated failed attempts. Locked accounts can only be unlocked after the customer passes identity verification. Never ask the customer for their full password or one-time security code. If the problem affects many customers at once, escalate it as a possible service incident instead of resetting accounts one by one.

## Card Blocking and Replacement
If a customer reports a lost, stolen, or compromised card, block the card immediately in the Card Management System, then order a replacement. Confirm the customer's identity before any change. If unauthorized transactions are visible, open a fraud case and follow the fraud reporting procedure. Do not give the customer the full card number during the call.

## Fraud Case Handling
When the Fraud Team receives a report, it reviews the account activity, contacts the customer through the registered phone number, and decides whether to block the account or the card. Employees must not contact the customer through a number or email provided in the suspicious message. The Fraud Team should acknowledge each report within 4 working hours and close the case after the final decision is recorded.

## Case Priority
P1: bank-wide outage or critical security or fraud incident. P2: major team or branch blocked with no workaround. P3: individual customer or employee blocked or degraded with limited workaround. P4: standard request, information request, or low-impact issue.
